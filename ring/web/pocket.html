Direction guide

Hold the phone upright, screen facing you, as if you are reading it. The back of the phone points the way you are looking. Flat, screen up, camera end pointing that same way, is the same heading. Do not turn the screen face down. Walking does not move the mark.

Practice without a compass

A real compass takes over once Safari provides it. Straight means within 25° of the marked direction. A compass reading worse than ±40° is ignored. A mark is stored only once the compass has settled: accuracy ±25° or better, and the recent readings within 15° of their average. Until then the page says the compass is still settling. Difference is target minus tilt-safe heading, positive to the right.
